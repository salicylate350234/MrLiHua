The School Garden Club

The Garden Club at Riverbend School began with a single raised bed behind the science building. At first, only six students met after class on Fridays. They planted tomatoes, beans, and sunflowers, and kept a notebook of what grew and what did not.

河湾学校的花园社团最初只是科学楼后面的一个种植箱。起初只有六名学生每周五放学后集合。他们种了番茄、豆子和向日葵，并记录下什么长得好、什么没长好。

The club's adviser, Ms. Chen, wanted the project to be more than a hobby. She asked students to test three kinds of soil and record how much water each bed received. The results were surprising: the bed with the least water produced the sweetest tomatoes. Students learned that more water does not always mean better growth.析

社团指导老师陈女士希望这个项目不只是一个爱好。她让学生测试三种土壤，并记录每块地浇了多少水。结果令人惊讶：水最少的那块地结出的番茄最甜。学生们由此明白，水多并不一定意味着长得更好。

Last spring, the club opened a small market on the school playground. They sold vegetables and homemade seed packets, and used the money to buy tools for the school's science lab. The market also gave younger students a chance to ask questions about growing food.

去年春天，社团在学校操场办了一个小市集。他们出售蔬菜和自制种子包，用收入为学校科学实验室购买工具。市集也让低年级学生有机会询问关于种植食物的问题。

This year, the club plans to build a rain barrel and a compost bin. Ms. Chen says the goal is not to produce the biggest harvest, but to help students notice the connections between soil, water, food, and their own choices.析

今年，社团计划建一个雨水桶和一个堆肥箱。陈女士说，目标不是收获最大，而是帮助学生发现土壤、水、食物和自己的选择之间的联系。
1 细节理解 B 答案页

What did the students discover about watering the beds?

原文依据
“The results were surprising: the bed with the least water produced the sweetest tomatoes.”
译：结果令人惊讶：水最少的那块地结出的番茄最甜。

least water 与 sweetest tomatoes 直接对应 B。

A与原文实验结果相反。
C原文没有说完全不浇水。
D原文没有说浇水会让土壤变差。
方法比较级 least/sweetest 是最直接的定位词。
2 原因细节 C 答案页

Why did the club open the market?

原文依据
“They sold vegetables and homemade seed packets, and used the money to buy tools for the school's science lab.”
译：他们出售蔬菜和自制种子包，用收入为学校科学实验室购买工具。

卖菜和种子包筹钱，给科学实验室买工具。

“The market also gave younger students a chance to ask questions about growing food.”

市集也让低年级学生来问种植问题，对应 share knowledge。

A市集卖的是蔬菜和种子包，不是工具。
B收入用于科学实验室工具，不是买种子。
D雨水桶和堆肥箱是今年的计划，不是市集目的。
方法题干问 purpose，答案要同时覆盖“筹钱”和“分享知识”两层。
